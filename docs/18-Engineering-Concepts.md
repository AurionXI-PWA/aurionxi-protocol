## Document Purpose

This document defines the engineering concepts and terminology used throughout the AurionXI documentation.

The concepts described here are documentation-specific definitions intended to provide consistent terminology across architectural documents, deployment methodology, governance, security, and protocol lifecycle documentation.

These terms describe the engineering approach adopted within the AurionXI project and should not be interpreted as established industry standards unless explicitly stated.

## Engineering Philosophy

AurionXI documentation intentionally defines engineering concepts that describe how the protocol is designed, deployed, validated, governed, and evolved.

The objective is to provide a common engineering vocabulary that improves consistency across documentation while making architectural discussions easier to understand.

# LDMA

Layered Domain-Oriented Multi-Diamond Architecture

Definition

LDMA describes the architectural organization of the AurionXI protocol.

The architecture separates protocol responsibilities into independent operational domains organized across multiple architectural layers while coordinating protocol behavior through CoreDiamond.

Engineering Objectives

• Explicit responsibility boundaries

• Independent protocol domains

• Modular evolution

• Architectural scalability

# SAG

Scoped Administrative Governance

Definition

SAG describes a planned governance direction in which administrative responsibilities may be delegated to independent protocol domains through explicitly defined authorization scopes.

Representative scopes may include Core, Treasury, Trading, Analytics, and AI domains.

Status

Planned architectural evolution.

Engineering Objectives

• Responsibility separation

• Administrative scalability

• Controlled protocol evolution

# VDPE

Verification-Driven Protocol Engineering

Definition

Verification-Driven Protocol Engineering is the engineering philosophy adopted throughout the AurionXI documentation.

The philosophy emphasizes that deployment alone is insufficient to establish production readiness.

Instead, architecture, deployment, configuration, governance, and operational behavior are independently verified before the protocol is considered production-ready.

Engineering Objectives

• Evidence-based engineering

• Verification before activation

• Operational confidence

• Engineering transparency
